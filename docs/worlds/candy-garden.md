# Vườn Kẹo Ngọt (Candy Garden) 🍭

- **Game**: Bunny Addition (`candy_garden` / `CandyGardenScreen`)
- **Educational goal**: addition — combining two groups into a total
- **Pattern**: VisualMathScreen (visual 3-choice)
- **Identity**: pastel pink sky, candy hills, lollipops, cupcake flowers,
  sugar sparkles; cheerful playful BGM; `candy` ambience (sparkles + bubble pops)
- **Objects**: 🍬 🍭 🍓 🧁 🍩 ⭐ (large, countable)
- **Levels**: tổng ≤ 5 (no timer) → ≤ 8 (30s) → ≤ 10 (25s, combo)
- **Stickers**: 🍭 Kẹo Mút · 🧁 Cupcake · 🍓 Dâu Ngọt · 🐰 Bunnine Kẹo Ngọt ·
  ⭐ Sao Kẹo · 👑 Vương Miện Kẹo (legendary)
- **Awards**: 🍬 Viên Kẹo Đầu Tiên · 🍭 Chuỗi Ngọt Ngào (5 streak) ·
  🧁 Bậc Thầy Kẹo Ngọt (3⭐ Màn 3)
- **Unlocks**: always available (entry world)
