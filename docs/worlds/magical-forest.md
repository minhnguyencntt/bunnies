# Rừng Diệu Kỳ (Magical Forest / Forest Adventure) 🌳

- **Game**: Bunny Subtraction (`forest_adventure` / `ForestAdventureScreen`)
- **Educational goal**: subtraction — taking away, counting what remains
- **Pattern**: VisualMathScreen (visual 3-choice); objects fly away with a soft
  whoosh; results always ≥ 1
- **Identity**: deep green forest glade, mushrooms, fireflies, butterflies,
  golden-hour light; nature BGM; `forest` ambience (wind, birds, sparkles)
- **Objects**: 🍄 🌰 🦋 ✨ 🌸 🐞
- **Levels**: ≤ 5 (no timer) → ≤ 8 (30s) → ≤ 10 (25s, combo)
- **Stickers**: 🍄 Nấm Xanh · 🦋 Bướm Rừng · ✨ Đom Đóm · 🐰 Bunnine Thám Hiểm ·
  ⭐ Sao Rừng · 🏆 Cúp Rừng Xanh (legendary)
- **Awards**: 🌲 Bước Vào Rừng · ✨ Đom Đóm Dẫn Đường (5 streak) ·
  🏹 Chúa Tể Rừng Xanh (3⭐ Màn 3)
- **Unlocks**: after Candy Garden Màn 1 (`unlockRequires`)
